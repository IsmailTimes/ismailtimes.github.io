I am an M.S. student in Geospatial Information Science & Technology at the University of Wyoming’s School of Computing, where I serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant. My research spans travel behavior, traffic safety, sustainable transportation planning, and urban digital twins. My thesis examines digital twin readiness and data equality in two Wyoming communities, Laramie and Afton.

Before graduate school I worked remotely for U.S. companies as a software development engineer in test, and I co-founded the web studio NextHunger IT.

Digital twin readiness & data equality in small cities

M.S. thesis

Compares a university town (Laramie) and a rural community (Afton) in Wyoming. Chapter 1 builds a Twin-Pole framework for when urban digital twins reproduce or reduce inequality; Chapter 2 studies data privacy, sharing, and equity in small-city digital twin governance.

Smart Rural Places project · Supervisor: Dr. Jason “Jake” Hawes · Project website

WYDOT school safety project

Fall 2025

Led GIS-based analysis with the Wyoming Department of Transportation to assess school-zone safety statewide, building ArcGIS Pro visualizations to identify high-risk traffic patterns.

Lead analyst · GIST 5200: Geographic Visualization
